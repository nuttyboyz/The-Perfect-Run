      Daily Challenges

      Three new challenges every day at midnight: one Easy, one Medium, and a Hard one (Elite every third day). PP is paid the moment you finish one. Runs the CPU plays and Duel Arena matches don't count.

      How to play

      Controls

      • W A S D Move · Shift Sprint · Space Jump · X Dash
      • Mouse Aim · Left click Attack
      • Q / G Element abilities · F Ultimate · Right click / C Aim, or block with a shield
      • 1 2 3 / Wheel Swap weapon · R Reload
      • Space again in the air: Web Swing (hold to hang on, let go to fly) · double-tap: Jetpack
      • E Open crate / use altar · B Place bed
      • Tab Shop · C Close camera · V First / third person · Esc Menu

      The run

      • 6 rounds, each a new generated map with its own boss. Round 6 ends with Thermes, a demonic viking deity.
      • There is no leaving early. Each round lasts 10 minutes; when the clock hits zero Death hunts you and the altar unseals. Hold E at the altar to summon the boss.
      • Kills pay 2 PC. Bosses pay 200 PC and let you pick 1 of 9 Deity Blessings.
      • Spend PC on crates (random power-ups), and beds (respawns).
      • You start with 1 bed. Place it, and you respawn there once.

      The Perfect Shop

      The Archivist trades in memories. Every blow, every round and every death leaves a crystal behind. Spend them, and your next run remembers.
