      Daily Challenges

      Three new challenges every day at midnight: one Easy, one Medium, and a Hard one (Elite every third day). PP is paid the moment you finish one. Runs the CPU plays and Duel Arena matches don't count.

      How to Play

      The world has gone quiet. Thermes, a horned viking deity, sits on its throne, and his monsters pour out of every graveyard, forest and frozen peak. Someone has to cut a path to him.

      1. 🛡️
        Choose your fighter. Pick a hero, an element to command, and three weapons from the Weapon Wall.
      2. ⚔️
        Slash and shoot. Waves of monsters close in from every side. Cut them down up close, gun them down from range, and burn, freeze or shock whatever is left.
      3. ⭐
        Level up. Every kill feeds your XP bar. Each new level hands you a power-up, so the longer you last, the harder you hit.
      4. 🪙
        Spend your coins. Fallen monsters drop Perfect Coins. Crack open crates for more power. Fall once and you get back up; the Perfect Shop sells Another Chance for more.
      5. 💀
        Beat the clock. Each round lasts ten minutes. When time runs out Death comes hunting and the altar wakes. Call the round's boss there and bring it down to earn a deity's blessing.
      6. 👑
        Face Thermes. Six rounds, six lands, six bosses. At the end he is waiting. Arrive strong enough to end him, and that is the perfect run.

      The Perfect Shop

      The Archivist trades in memories. Every blow, every round and every death leaves a crystal behind. Spend them, and your next run remembers.
